Privacy policy

MyBooks (Android app com.mcosta.mybooks.ng, and its iPhone version) · last updated 6 November 2026

MyBooks keeps your library on your phone. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold. This page explains what stays on the phone, what the app sends over the internet to work, and what the optional community stores.

On your phone

Your books and everything about them stay on your phone: reading status and dates, scores, progress, notes, reviews, quotes, tags, series, loans (including the names of the people you lend books to), cover photos you take, reading challenges and settings.

  • Phone backup. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android copies the library, settings and cover photos to your Google account (iPhone: iCloud), so they come back on a new phone. This is your phone's own backup, run by Google or Apple; the developer has no access to it.
  • Files you export. Backups and Goodreads files go only where you choose to save or share them.

What the app sends to work

  • Book details and covers. To fill in a book, the app sends its ISBN, or its title and author, to Google Books, Open Library and the Biblioteca Nacional de Portugal, and loads cover pictures from them. Book suggestions and "More by the author" send author names and subjects to Google Books and Open Library. The author panel loads biographies from Wikipedia. Nothing that identifies you is sent with these requests.
  • New in Portugal. The monthly lists of new books are downloaded from GitHub Pages. If you turn on the monthly notification, the phone checks for a new list once a day and compares it with your library on the phone; your library is not sent anywhere.
  • Barcodes and photos of pages. Barcodes are read on the phone by Google Play services' code scanner. Photos of pages for quotes are read on the phone (ML Kit text recognition); the photo is neither uploaded nor kept, only the text you choose to save.
  • Bookshops. "Where to buy" opens the bookshop's website in your browser with the book's ISBN or title; from then on that website's own privacy policy applies.

All of these connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

The community (optional)

You can use MyBooks without an account. If you join the community to share your reading with friends, these are stored on the community server (hosted by Supabase):

  • your email address, used only to send you sign-in codes;
  • your username, display name, profile photo (if you add one) and who can see your profile;
  • for each book you share: title, authors, ISBN, cover link, status, score, progress and your review (your private notes are never shared, and you can mark any book "Don't share");
  • your activity (books started and finished), likes, friends, friend requests and blocked people;
  • reports you send about other members, and reports about you.

What you share is visible to your friends, or to everyone if you make your profile public. It is used only to run the community, and is not given or sold to anyone.

For the "Readers like you loved" suggestions, the app sends the community server the ISBNs of up to 50 of your favourite books; they are used for that search and not stored.

Deleting your data

  • Your library: delete books in the app, or uninstall it (Android also offers "Clear storage").
  • Your community account: in the app, go to Settings › Community › your account and choose Delete account. Your profile, photo, shared books, activity, likes, friends and blocks are deleted straight away. Reports you sent are kept without your name; reports about you keep your username, so that abuse can't be erased by deleting an account. You can also ask for deletion without the app.

Children

MyBooks is not directed at children under 13, and the community is not meant for them.
