# BookNerd Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A BookNerd chat, opened from Home, that suggests books (existing engine), suggests friends (new server function) and writes a book abstract from five answers (templates) — no AI, nothing to pay.

**Architecture:** Pure pieces first — keyword understanding (`understand`), the abstract templates (`writeAbstract`) and the reader reasons — then a `BookNerdViewModel` that runs the three flows over a list of chat messages, and a Compose screen. One new `security definer` SQL function returns suggested readers.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin Multiplatform (commonMain), Compose Multiplatform, supabase-kt, Postgres (pgTAP in CI).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-booknerd-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No AI or language model anywhere; book suggestions and abstracts run on the phone and send nothing.
- Keywords (lower-cased, accents removed, whole word or word start):
  Books: livro, livros, ler, leitura, sugere, sugestao, recomenda, book, books, read, suggest, recommend.
  Friends: amigo, amigos, amiga, pessoas, leitores, gente, friend, friends, people, readers, buddies.
  Abstract: resumo, sinopse, ideia, enredo, historia, abstract, blurb, synopsis, idea, plot, story.
  Most hits wins; on a tie FRIENDS beats ABSTRACT beats BOOKS.
- Portuguese when the text has a Portuguese keyword or one of ã õ ç é ê á à ó ô í ú; otherwise English. Chips stay in English.
- Copy (EN / PT): greeting "Olá! I'm BookNerd. I can suggest books, find you fellow readers, or write an abstract for a book idea."; chips "Suggest books", "Suggest friends", "Write an abstract"; fallback "I can help with three things:"; "Here are some books you might like:" / "Aqui vão alguns livros que podes gostar:"; "That's all I have for now." / "Por agora é tudo."; "Add a few books you loved and I'll find more like them." / "Junta alguns livros de que gostaste e eu encontro mais parecidos."; "Join the community to get friend suggestions"; "I couldn't find anyone yet — invite your friends!" / "Ainda não encontrei ninguém — convida os teus amigos!"; "Couldn't reach the community." / "Não consegui chegar à comunidade."; reasons "Also loved {title} and {n} more" / "Também adorou {title} e mais {n}", "Friend of {a} and {b}" / "Amigo de {a} e {b}" (", and {n} more" / " e mais {n}" beyond two); "Tell me a little more?" / "Conta-me um pouco mais?".
- Abstract questions (EN / PT): 1 genre "What genre is it?" / "De que género é?" with chips Romance, Mystery, Fantasy, Science fiction, Historical, Thriller, Drama, Comedy; 2 "Who is the main character? (a name and a few words)" / "Quem é a personagem principal? (um nome e algumas palavras)"; 3 "Where and when does it happen?" / "Onde e quando acontece?"; 4 "What goes wrong, or what do they want?" / "O que corre mal, ou o que quer a personagem?"; 5 tone "What tone?" / "Que tom?" with chips Light, Dark, Moving, Funny, Suspenseful.
- Engine failure (EN / PT): "I couldn't load suggestions right now." / "Não consegui carregar sugestões agora."
- Abstract: 120–180 words; at least 3 template variants per genre × language; buttons "Another version", "Copy", "Share", "Start over".
- Books: 5 per page; chips "More", "Something else". Friends: up to 10; buttons "Add friend" → "Request sent", "View profile".
- `suggested_readers(max int default 10)`, max capped at 20; order `shared_books + 2 * mutual_friends` desc, then display name; excludes self, any `friendships` row with the caller (any status), blocked either way; taste only from public profiles that share activity; ratings 4–5 on both sides, matched by ISBN.
- Gate: `export JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS=-Djavax.net.ssl.trustStoreType=Windows-ROOT && ./gradlew :releases:test :shared:compileCommonMainKotlinMetadata :shared:testAndroidHostTest :androidReader:testDebugUnitTest :androidApp:testDebugUnitTest :androidApp:assembleDebug -q`, then `./gradlew :androidReader:lintDebug :androidApp:lintDebug -q`; pgTAP runs in the `supabase` CI workflow (no Docker locally).
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. During the abstract questions, a typed answer that contains a keyword ("a girl who loves books") is an answer, not a new request (Task 5 test `abstractAnswersAreNotIntents`).
2. Very long answers (a 400-character "character") keep the abstract readable: each answer is cut to 120 characters at a word (Task 4 test `longAnswersAreCut`).
3. Tapping "Add friend" twice sends one request and the button shows "Request sent" (Task 5 test `addFriendOnce`).
4. Tapping "More" twice quickly shows each book once (Task 5 test `moreTwiceNoDuplicates`).
5. The suggestion engine failing (exception) gives a friendly line, not a crash (Task 5 test `bookEngineFailing`).
