Delete your community account

MyBooks NG · the app's library on your phone is not affected

In the app

  1. Open MyBooks NG and go to Settings.
  2. Under Community, tap your account to open Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Send an email to mario.rui.dacosta@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, asking for your account to be deleted, and say your username. Writing from that address shows the account is yours. It is deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • your account and email address, username, display name and profile photo;
  • the books you shared, with their scores, progress and reviews;
  • your activity, likes, friends, friend requests and blocks.

What is kept

  • Reports you sent about other members are kept without your name.
  • Reports about you keep your username, so that abuse can't be erased by deleting an account.
  • Your library on your phone stays as it is. To remove it too, uninstall the app.

Nothing else about you is kept. See the privacy policy.
